Chef Kams is a Botswana catering team built on one idea — great food brings people together. We cook local meat over fire, smoke and cure in-house, and finish with patisserie made from scratch.

Whether it's a company year-end for hundreds or a private dinner for eight in a concession three hours from tar, the standard doesn't move.
