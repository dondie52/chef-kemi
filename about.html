About Chef Kams

Food · People · Memories

Exceptional food. Unforgettable experiences. That is the whole brief, and it has not changed since the first event.

Great food brings people together.

Chef Kams is a Botswana catering team built on one idea. We cook local meat over fire, smoke and cure in-house, and finish with patisserie made from scratch.

Whether it is a company year-end for hundreds or a private dinner for eight in a concession three hours from tar, the standard does not move.

We work anywhere in Botswana, and travel across the border to COUNTRIES_TODO for safari lodges and destination events — including sites with no road access, no power and no running water.
